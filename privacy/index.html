Privacy.

Your audio goes to the provider you choose. Your transcripts stay in places you control.

Recording access

You approve a folder through Android’s folder picker. The app reads enabled recording sources within that grant. Folder imports do not need microphone access. The optional first-recording trial requests microphone permission only after you tap Record, records while this app is visible, and stops when you tap Stop or after 30 seconds. Cancel or leaving the app before saving discards the unsaved trial without uploading it. A saved trial is sent to your selected transcription provider and remains in private app storage until uninstall. Contacts, call-log, Accessibility, device-admin and all-files permissions are not requested. Original recordings from other apps are never modified or deleted.

Transcription and local storage

Eligible new finalized recordings are sent directly from your phone to your selected Groq or OpenAI API using your key. Long audio is split into bounded chunks. The provider receives audio and applies its own privacy, retention and billing policies. No recording or transcript is sent to a Ramble server.

Your phone stores processing history, encrypted API credentials, temporary working audio and transcripts. Keys are encrypted using Android Keystore. App backup and device transfer are disabled. Transcripts are exported to Downloads or a folder you authorize. This app includes no advertising or analytics SDK and does not log audio, transcript bodies or API keys.

Google user data

Google Drive is optional. If you authorize it, Ramble creates a normal folder named Ramble Transcripts (or reuses its existing connected folder) and uploads generated transcript text. The app requests only drive.file: permission to see, create and manage the specific files created or used with this app. It does not request access to your entire Drive, scan unrelated files or upload source audio to Drive.

Google authorization is managed through Google Play services. There is no Ramble server-side token exchange. Google user data is used only for the Drive export you enable; it is not sold, used for advertising or provided to Ramble for model training. Generated text is stored in your Google account under Google’s policies.

Your AI assistants

Giving ChatGPT, Claude or another assistant access to Drive is a separate action under that assistant’s permissions and policies. Copying the suggested prompt does not grant access. The app does not automatically connect an assistant, send it your files or guarantee that it has indexed them.

Pause, revoke or delete

You can pause processing, disable recording sources, disconnect Drive or revoke Android/Google access to stop future processing or export. Disconnecting does not delete existing user-owned files. Uninstall removes app-private data; exported Downloads and Drive files can remain. Delete those copies separately if desired. Content already sent to a transcription provider follows that provider’s retention policy.

Existing baseline recordings are excluded. Unsupported, incomplete or undated files wait without upload. An uncertain provider request may require approval before retry to avoid duplicate billing.

Website and contact
