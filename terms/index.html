Terms & usage.

The standalone edition

Ramble uses your own Groq or OpenAI API key. You manage that provider account, its charges, quotas and terms. Optional Drive export requires your Google authorization. This edition has no Ramble subscription, account or hosted transcription service.

Alpha software

This build is provided for testing, without a guaranteed processing time, transcription accuracy or device compatibility. Keep original recordings and check important details against them. Android can delay background work; unsupported files may remain waiting. The app does not make recordings itself or change Samsung’s call-recording behavior.

Your recordings

Only process recordings you are entitled to use and send to your chosen provider. You control recording permissions and any separate access granted to AI assistants.

Open source

The source distribution’s license and third-party notices govern the code. They do not grant rights to unrelated trademarks or providers. Public release links will be added to the product page when published.

Read the standalone app privacy policy before enabling processing or Drive export. For questions, contact the project maintainer.
