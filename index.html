Your AI remembers.

Your recordings become text your assistants can find.

Keep using Samsung’s built-in recorder. Ramble automatically transcribes new supported recordings with your own Groq or OpenAI API key and saves the text on your phone. Optionally connect Google Drive to make those transcripts available to your existing AI assistants.

How your data is handled

Set up once. Record as usual.

  1. Enter your Groq or OpenAI API key.
  2. Allow access to your recording folder. Optionally include saved phone-call recordings, where your device supports them.
  3. Connect Google Drive if you want automatic transcript export. Ramble creates its own folder; it does not read your unrelated Drive files.

Your phone. Your key. Your files.

No Ramble account, Ramble transcription server or computer is needed. Audio goes directly to your chosen transcription provider. Only generated text is uploaded to Drive, after your authorization. An AI assistant needs its own Drive connection; the app provides instructions you can copy.

Currently in testing

The signed Android alpha has emulator validation; real Samsung compatibility is still being tested. Completed supported AAC/M4A recordings are processed automatically. Unsupported or incomplete files wait. Background work is periodic and can be delayed by Android. Existing recordings present at setup are excluded. An optional first-recording trial uses the microphone after you tap Record and grant access. It is limited to 30 seconds and does not record phone calls.

Public APK/source download links will be added when the Android release is published. Hosted Ramble transcription is not available in this standalone edition.
